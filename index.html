Piezowalk

Harvesting Energy from Every Step

Problem & Solution

⚡ The Problem

As cities grow, public infrastructure consumes increasing amounts of energy — most of it still sourced from non-renewable means. The energy from daily human movement, especially in crowded spaces, remains untapped, while sustainability goals demand innovative solutions.

🌍 Our Solution

Piezowalk transforms footsteps into clean energy using piezoelectric mats placed in high-traffic areas. Each step generates electrical power that can be used to light spaces or power sensors, contributing to a smarter, greener urban ecosystem.
