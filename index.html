Piezowalk

Harvesting energy from every step — practical piezoelectric mats for public spaces.

Problem & Solution

⚠️

The Problem

Modern public spaces consume large amounts of electricity while the kinetic energy produced by people moving through these spaces is wasted. This missed opportunity continues dependency on non-renewables and increases operating costs.

🔋

The Solution

Piezowalk integrates piezoelectric mats into high footfall areas to convert mechanical pressure into electrical energy. The harvested power can support lighting, sensors, and micro-systems — reducing demand and boosting resilience.
