Problem & Solution

Problem Statement

Public infrastructure faces rising energy consumption and limited adoption of sustainable practices, leading to inefficiencies and missed opportunities for reducing environmental impact and operational costs.

Solution

Piezoelectricity, which generates electrical energy from mechanical movements, offers a promising solution. By using piezoelectric tiles in heavy foot traffic areas, we reduce energy consumption and move toward greener, smarter cities.
