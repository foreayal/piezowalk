Problem Statement & Solution

Problem Statement

Public infrastructure faces rising energy consumption and limited adoption of sustainable practices, leading to inefficiencies and missed opportunities for reducing environmental impact and operational costs.

Solution

Piezoelectricity, which generates electrical energy from mechanical movements, offers a promising solution. By using piezoelectric tiles in heavy foot traffic areas, we can reduce energy consumption and move a step forward toward green and smart cities.
