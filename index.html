Piezowalk

Harvesting Energy from Every Step

Problem & Solution

Problem

Modern public spaces consume enormous amounts of energy, but much of the energy around us—like the mechanical pressure from footsteps—goes completely unused. This results in wasted potential and continued reliance on non-renewable power sources.

Solution

Piezowalk transforms everyday foot traffic into clean electricity through piezoelectric materials embedded within mats. These mats harness mechanical pressure and convert it into energy, promoting sustainability and supporting greener cities.
