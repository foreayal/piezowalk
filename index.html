About the Project

Piezowalk is an innovative energy-harvesting system that transforms the mechanical energy of footsteps into electrical power. Designed for public spaces like malls, stations, and walkways, Piezowalk mats provide a renewable and eco-friendly way to contribute toward green technology and zero-carbon goals. Every step taken generates usable energy while also gathering real-time data through IoT integration.
