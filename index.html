Harvesting Energy from Every Step

Problem & Solution

⚡ The Problem

Public spaces consume enormous energy — yet, we walk past endless opportunities. The kinetic energy from footsteps in malls, stations, and walkways is wasted daily. With rising costs and global warming, inaction means unsustainability.

🌍 Our Solution

Piezowalk harnesses every step. Using piezoelectric mats, we convert mechanical motion into electricity to power LEDs, sensors, or microcontrollers — turning movement into renewable energy and paving the path toward smarter, greener cities.
