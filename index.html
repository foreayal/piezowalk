IMPLEMENTATION OF PIEZO TILES IN PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURES

Harvesting energy from every step — practical piezoelectric mats for public spaces.

Problem & Solution

The Problem

Modern public spaces consume large amounts of electricity while kinetic energy from pedestrian movement is wasted, increasing reliance on non-renewables and costs.

The Solution

Piezowalk integrates piezoelectric mats in high-footfall areas to convert mechanical pressure into electrical energy for lighting, sensors, and micro-systems — reducing demand and improving sustainability.

What is Piezotile?

Piezotile was our earlier prototype — a rigid piezoelectric tile that captured energy from footsteps. Although functional, its installation and scalability were limited. This led us to innovate the flexible Piezomat design, ideal for high-traffic public spaces like malls, stations, and walkways.
