What is Piezowalk? 🧠

Piezowalk is a piezoelectric energy-harvesting mat designed for high-foot-traffic areas such as shopping malls, train stations, and public walkways. It converts mechanical pressure from footsteps into electrical energy using piezoelectric materials embedded in a flexible mat structure. The goal is to integrate clean energy generation seamlessly into everyday movement while supporting Malaysia’s zero-carbon and green technology initiatives.

How It Works ⚙️

When pedestrians walk on the Piezowalk mat, the mechanical stress generated on the piezoelectric materials produces an electric charge. The generated current is stored or used to power nearby systems like LED displays or sensors. Our IoT system monitors the energy harvested in real time for data analysis and optimization.

Data & Results 📊

The graph and data collected from our IoT system show how much energy is harvested with varying foot traffic. Each step generates a small but measurable voltage, proving the system’s efficiency for real-world use.

Experiments 🎞️

We conducted several tests on the piezomat’s durability and voltage output under different weights and step frequencies. Results showed that a consistent footfall rate produces an optimal charge accumulation curve.
