What is Piezowalk? 🧠

Piezowalk is a piezoelectric energy-harvesting mat designed for high pedestrian areas such as malls, stations, and walkways. It converts footsteps into usable electricity, promoting sustainability and green innovation.

How It Works ⚙️

When pressure is applied to the piezoelectric material within the mat, it generates an electric charge due to the piezoelectric effect. This charge is then stored or used directly for powering small systems or lighting.
