How It Works

Each step compresses piezoelectric layers, converting mechanical stress into electrical energy. The generated energy is then rectified and stored for low-power uses like lighting and sensors.

What is Piezotile?

Piezotile was our original rigid piezoelectric tile that inspired the evolution of the Piezomat — a flexible, easier-to-install, and more efficient mat system for public spaces.
